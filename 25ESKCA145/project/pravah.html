About the fest

One current. Every discipline.

Pravah — meaning "flow" — is SKIT Jaipur's flagship fest where technical rigor and creative energy run in the same channel. From hackathons to battle of bands, from robotics arenas to nukkad natak, every event feeds into a single day of momentum on 10th July 2026.

Open to students from every college. Come solo, come as a squad — pick your events, register online and walk in with your pass ready.

Why "Pravah"?

A river doesn't ask permission to move — it finds a way through anything in front of it. That's the spirit of this fest: ideas, performances and energy flowing freely across every stage on campus, for one day, together.
